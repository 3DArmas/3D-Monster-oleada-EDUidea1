# Feature: Warehouse map (recreate the isometric concept in Unity)

## Objective
Rebuild the industrial warehouse arena from the approved Higgsfield concept art as a playable Unity map, using the existing `RPG_FPS_game_assets_industrial` pack. No Higgsfield credits are spent.

## Problem / why
The current arena is a primitive blockout. The user approved the concept art (isometric overview + eye-level interior) and wants the in-game map to match its layout and mood.

## Reference images (concept art, Higgsfield)
- Isometric overview: job `71058351-82f2-48cf-8c87-04d7f8ef40b2`
- Eye-level interior: job `118ff53d-1f06-427b-bdb3-3179b5a619bb`

## Layout spec (north = back wall of the isometric image, player start at origin)
- Square warehouse, ~34 x 34 m interior, perimeter brick/concrete walls ~6 m high with windows.
- North: full-width steel mezzanine (~3.5 m high, ~4 m deep) with yellow railings, two staircases and one ladder (decorative) between them, crates on top.
- West: two long rusty shipping containers running north-south forming two corridors (container-container and container-wall), with a small railed platform on top of their north end, reachable by stairs.
- East: large open roll-up loading door near the north-east corner with yellow bollards and hazard stripes; an interior partition wall running north-south forms an east corridor with crates.
- South-east corner: enclosed merchant room with tarp canopy and shelves, turquoise light (shop location).
- South-west corner: small walled room with a wall breach.
- Center: open combat zone with scattered cover (crate stacks, pallets, jersey barriers, barrels), a lamp post, a forklift-like prop.
- Six zombie spawn points at wall breaches: NW corner, north wall center, west wall middle, east wall middle, south wall center, SW room.
- Lighting: night. Orange sodium lamps along walls, red emergency lights near breaches, cold moonlight, exponential fog, URP post-processing (bloom, vignette, teal/orange grading). Partial broken roof with an overhead crane and hanging chains if performance allows.

## Constraints
- Non-destructive: build into a NEW scene `Assets/Scenes/Warehouse.unity` copied from `SampleScene.unity` (keeps Managers, Canvas, player, HordeDirector wiring); `SampleScene` stays untouched.
- Map is generated by an Editor builder script (reviewable, re-runnable) runnable from a menu and from batchmode `-executeMethod`.
- Performance: 60 fps at 1080p with 24 zombies; PC renderer is Forward+; few shadow-casting lights.
- Colliders must match visual shapes (see past lessons: no SphereCollider on non-uniform scale; use Box or convex Mesh).
- NavMesh must connect every spawn to the center; no test zombies left in the scene.
- Scene changes in their own commit; local commits only, no push.

## Implementation notes
- Files: `Assets/Editor/WarehouseMapBuilder.cs` (+ `.Shell.cs`, `.Interior.cs`, `.Lighting.cs`, partial class, layout constants at the top) and `Assets/Editor/WarehouseMapVerifier.cs`.
- Entry points: menu `Tools/Outbreak/Build Warehouse Map`; batch `-executeMethod Outbreak.EditorTools.WarehouseMapBuilder.BuildFromCommandLine`; verification `-executeMethod Outbreak.EditorTools.WarehouseMapVerifier.Run -shotsDir <dir>`.
- Prefab bounds were measured with a throw-away probe (Renderer bounds) before placing anything; pack props are ground-snapped from their measured bounds (`P(...)` helper). No Editor asmdef exists, so `Assets/Editor` compiles into `Assembly-CSharp-Editor`.
- Primitive-built: perimeter walls (tiled pack brick texture), mezzanine + platform + stairs + ladder + rails, partition walls, merchant room, forklift, lamp post, roof trusses/panels, overhead crane with chains, windows, roll-up door, bollards.
- New materials live in `Assets/Materials/Warehouse` (URP Lit, instancing on). Post-processing uses a COPY of the sample profile (`Assets/Settings/WarehouseProfile.asset`), so `SampleScene` is untouched.
- Old arena (`TERRENO`, `Obstaculos`) is deactivated in `Warehouse.unity`, not deleted. Player moved to (0, 1.1, -2); `ShopCube` rests at (14.6, 1.25, -13.9) in the merchant room; the 4 existing spawn points were reused and 2 added, all 6 assigned to `ZombieSpawner.spawnPoints` through `SerializedObject`.
- NavMesh: `NavMeshSurface` on `Warehouse_Map` (children only, physics colliders), data saved to `Assets/Scenes/Warehouse/NavMesh-Warehouse.asset`. Stairs are walkable through an invisible ramp collider (32 deg) under the visual steps; ladder is decorative.
- The scene is NOT added to Build Settings (it lists only `SampleScene`; adding it would change load order, so left to the user).

## Known issues / not verified
- Not run in Play mode (batchmode rule): CharacterController climbing the stairs, zombie behaviour on the new NavMesh and real-time frame rate (24 zombies, 41 point lights in Forward+) are verified only through NavMesh queries, a physics ray profile and light counts.
- `ShopInteractable` re-places the cube in front of the player whenever the shop opens (existing behaviour, not changed); the merchant room is its resting place and a visual hub, not a forced location.
- Roof is partial by design (moon shafts through gaps); the moon disc is visible through the roof gaps only from some angles (`16_eye_moon_direction.png`).
- Concept deltas: containers are 8 m prefabs stretched 2.5x along their length (20 m) to match the concept; wall breaches are rectangular pockets with rubble rather than organic holes; the forklift, lamp post, mezzanine, stairs, rails, ladder, bollards, roll-up door, crane and chains are primitives (pack has none).
